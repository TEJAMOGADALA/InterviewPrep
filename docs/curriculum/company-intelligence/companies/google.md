# 2. Company Overview

## 2.1 Company Profile

Google is a global technology and product company with major engineering organizations spanning search, advertising, cloud infrastructure, artificial intelligence, developer platforms, distributed systems, mobile, security, and consumer products.

For PrepOS, Google should be modeled primarily as a **high-bar engineering organization** where technical problem solving, coding ability, engineering judgment, scalability awareness, communication, and increasing levels of technical influence become progressively more important with seniority.

# 4. Hiring Philosophy

## 4.1 Hiring Philosophy

The supplied research describes Google's SWE evaluation as holistic rather than purely coding-based.

A candidate should not be modeled as interview-ready merely because they can solve algorithmic problems.

PrepOS should evaluate readiness across:

- Problem solving
- Coding
- Technical depth
- System design
- Communication
- Leadership
- Ownership
- Learning ability
- Ambiguity handling
- Engineering judgment
